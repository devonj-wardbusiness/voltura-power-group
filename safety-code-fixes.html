What we handle

Code violations we fix every day

From minor permit issues to full rewires, we handle the full range of electrical code compliance work in El Paso County.

⚠

Failed Inspection Repairs

Inspector left a red tag? We review the report, correct every violation, and coordinate the re-inspection so you can close on time.

🔌

Knob & Tube Wiring Removal

Uninsulated, ungrounded, and unsafe — knob-and-tube is a fire and insurance risk. We remove it fully and replace with modern NM-B cable.

⚡

Aluminum Wiring Remediation

Aluminum branch circuit wiring requires CO/ALR devices or pigtail splices at every connection. We do it right — not just the quick fix.

🔒

AFCI / GFCI Upgrades

Modern NEC requires arc-fault and ground-fault protection in bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms, garages, and more. We bring you up to current code.

💾

Unpermitted Work Correction

Previous owner DIY'd the garage panel or added circuits without a permit? We assess, document, correct deficiencies, and get it legalized.

💡

Overloaded Panel & Circuit Fixes

Double-tapped breakers, undersized feeders, and overloaded circuits are code violations and fire hazards. We correct them and document everything.

Red flags

Signs your wiring isn't up to code

  • ⚠ Inspector flagged violations at closing
  • ⚠ Home is pre-1980s with original wiring
  • ⚠ Two-prong (ungrounded) outlets throughout
  • ⚠ No GFCI in bathrooms, kitchen, or garage
  • ⚠ Breakers that trip repeatedly or won't reset
  • ⚠ Breaker box has more breakers than spaces
  • ⚠ Wires spliced in walls without junction boxes
  • ⚠ Insurance company requiring electrical update
  • ⚠ Smoke or burning smell near outlets or panel
  • ⚠ Lights flicker when appliances turn on
  • ⚠ Extension cords used as permanent wiring
  • ⚠ Panel brand flagged as defective (Zinsco, FPE)
  • ⚠ New addition or remodel added without permits
  • ⚠ Homebuyer inspection report has electrical items
