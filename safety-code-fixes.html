What we fix

Common safety and code issues, done properly.

GFCI & AFCI Corrections

Required in bathrooms, kitchens, garages, and exterior locations. We bring existing wiring into compliance without unnecessary gut-jobs.

Grounding & Bonding

Missing or improper grounds are a real shock hazard. We trace the issue and correct it at the source — not just at the outlet.

Smoke & CO Detection

Interconnected detector placement to meet current code. Hardwired or battery-backup options depending on your setup.

Hazard Identification

We document what we find and give you options — what's critical now, what can wait, and what the cost is. No forced upgrades.

Signs you may need safety work

✅ Outlets in bathrooms, kitchen, or garage have no GFCI protection
✅ Outlet tester shows "hot/neutral reversed" or "open ground"
✅ Older home (pre-1990s) with original wiring never updated
✅ Home inspector or insurance flagged electrical issues
✅ Smoke detectors are more than 10 years old
✅ Getting ready to sell and want the electrical clean before listing
