Cimarron Hills homes need an electrician who knows older wiring.

Built primarily in the 1970s and 1980s, Cimarron Hills homes often carry the electrical hallmarks of that era: 100-amp service, aluminum branch circuit wiring, two-prong outlets, and panels that are well past their expected lifespan.

These aren't just cosmetic issues — they're fire and safety risks. We assess, document, and correct them properly, and we pull El Paso County permits on every job so your work is legal and insurable.
