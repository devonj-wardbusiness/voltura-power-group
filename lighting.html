What We Install

Every type of lighting,
done right.

💡
Recessed Lighting

New recessed cans in existing ceilings or new construction. Properly spaced, correctly wired, with clean trim rings that sit flush — no gaps, no sag.

⚡
LED Upgrades

Retrofit existing fixtures to LED for better light quality and lower energy bills. We replace the whole fixture or just the driver depending on what makes sense.

🔌
Under-Cabinet Lighting

Hardwired under-cabinet lighting for kitchens and workspaces. Clean wire routing, no visible cords, switched independently from overhead lighting.

📷
Smart Switches & Dimmers

Lutron, Leviton, or your brand of choice — we wire smart switches correctly, including neutral wire requirements that most homes need addressed.

🌳
Landscape & Exterior Lighting

Hardwired path lights, soffit lighting, security floods, and outlet additions for outdoor spaces. Weatherproof installs that last Colorado winters.

💧
Ceiling Fans & Fixtures

New ceiling fan installs, fixture replacements, and junction box upgrades. Balanced, properly braced, and wired for the fan's control type.
