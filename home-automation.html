    FAQ

    Smart lighting
    questions, answered.

    Can one switch really control lights in every room?

    Yes. With a smart lighting system and a centralized keypad or control panel, you can set up a single switch — by your bed, front door, or wherever makes sense — to control or check every light in the house. No more walking out to the garage to check if the light's still on.

    Do I need to rewire my whole house for smart lighting?

    Usually not. Most smart switches and dimmers install in your existing switch boxes, though some require a neutral wire that older homes may be missing — we check this during your quote and handle any wiring updates needed.

    What's a lighting "scene" and how does it work?

    A scene is a saved combination of lights, brightness levels, and on/off states triggered by one button or app tap — for example, "Movie Night" dims the living room and turns off the kitchen, or "Away" shuts everything down and arms exterior lights on a schedule.

    Will smart switches work with my existing light fixtures?

    In most cases, yes. Smart switches and dimmers replace your existing wall switches and work with standard LED, incandescent, and most dimmable fixtures. We confirm compatibility with your specific fixtures before installing.
