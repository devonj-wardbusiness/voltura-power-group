What we do in Falcon

Electrical services built for Falcon homes

Panel Upgrades

EV Charger Install

Dedicated Circuits

Why Falcon homeowners call us

Built for Falcon’s newer, larger homes.

Falcon's housing stock is largely newer construction — 2000s through today — with larger square footage, bigger panels, and more electrical demand than older Colorado Springs homes. That means EV chargers, hot tubs, and outbuilding sub-panels are the norm, not the exception.

We're also familiar with acreage properties that need long runs to shops, barns, or detached garages. We size wire correctly and pull El Paso County permits every time.
