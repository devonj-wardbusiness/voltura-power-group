What We Diagnose

Common issues we
find and fix.

⚡
Tripping Breakers

Breakers that trip repeatedly signal an overloaded circuit, a fault, or a failing breaker. We find the cause — not just reset and leave.

🔋
Dead Outlets

Could be a tripped GFCI, a wiring fault, or a failed outlet. We trace the circuit and restore power correctly.

💡
Flickering Lights

Flickering when appliances run often points to a loose connection, undersized wiring, or a panel issue. We diagnose the real source.

🔥
Burning Smells

A burning or fishy smell from an outlet or panel is a potential fire hazard. We treat this as same-day priority — call us immediately.

🔌
Weird Voltage / Surges

Lights that dim when the AC kicks on, appliances browning out — we check service voltage, neutral connections, and panel condition.

🚫
Partial Outages

One circuit or area of the house losing power while others stay on. We trace the fault and restore full service safely.

Don’t Wait

Warning signs that need
same-day attention.

⚠Burning or fishy smell from any outlet, switch, or your panel
⚠Sparks when plugging something in
⚠Outlet or switch that feels warm or hot to the touch
⚠Breaker that won't reset or trips immediately after resetting
⚠Buzzing or crackling sound from the panel or walls
