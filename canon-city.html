What we do in Cañon City

Full-service electrical for Cañon City homes & businesses

⚠

Safety & Code Fixes

Cañon City's older neighborhoods have homes with wiring that predates modern safety codes. Knob-and-tube, aluminum branch wiring, and fuse boxes are all common. We correct them properly.

⚡

Panel Upgrades

100A to 200A upgrades, sub-panel installs, and service entrance replacements. All Fremont County permitted and coordinated with the utility.

🔌

EV Charger Install

Level 2 EV charger installs for all makes. We assess your panel first and upgrade if needed. Permit and inspection handled for every job.

📌

Dedicated Circuits

Hot tubs, shop tools, garage heaters, HVAC systems — we run dedicated circuits safely and to code for any high-draw application.

🔎

Troubleshooting

Tripping breakers, dead outlets, partial outages — $65 service call with diagnostic included. We find the issue and fix it same day when possible.

📌

Generator & Backup Power

Standby generator installs with automatic transfer switches. Rural Fremont County properties benefit most — power keeps running no matter what.

We make the drive

Cañon City deserves licensed, permitted electrical work.

Many contractors don't want to make the drive to Cañon City. We do. And when we get there, we do the job the same way we do it everywhere else — licensed work, pulled permits, passed inspections, and documentation you can show your insurance company.

Cañon City has both a city permit office and Fremont County for work outside city limits. We know which applies to your property and handle the paperwork start to finish.
