What we install

Every situation is different — we'll spec what's right for your home and loads.

Standby Generator Hookup

Electrical connection for natural gas or propane standby generators (Generac, Kohler, Briggs & Stratton, etc.). Includes load calculations and transfer switch wiring.

Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS)

Whole-home or critical-load ATS that senses a power outage and starts the generator automatically. No manual steps required.

Manual Interlock

A code-compliant interlock kit on your existing panel that safely allows a portable generator to feed your home circuits without backfeeding the grid.

Load-Shedding Circuits

Identify and wire the circuits that matter most — HVAC, refrigerator, medical equipment, well pump — so your generator runs only what's needed.

Is a generator right for you?

✅ You've lost power more than once in the past year
✅ You rely on medical equipment that can't go down
✅ You have a well pump, sump pump, or livestock operation
✅ You work from home and can't afford downtime
✅ Colorado weather (ice, wind, wildfire seasons) affects your area
✅ You're buying or building and want backup built in from the start
