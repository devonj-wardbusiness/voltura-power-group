What we install

Backup power solutions we specialize in

We handle every step — sizing, placement, gas line coordination, transfer switch wiring, and final inspection. You don't manage a single trade.

⚡

Standby Generator Install

Permanent, automatic standby generators that start themselves within 10 seconds of an outage. We handle siting, electrical, and gas line tie-in coordination.

🔄

Automatic Transfer Switch

The ATS is the brain of your backup system. We wire it correctly so utility and generator power never mix — protecting your equipment and the grid workers outside.

🏠

Whole-Home Backup

Full-load transfer means every circuit in your home stays live during an outage — HVAC, refrigerator, medical equipment, lighting, and security systems.

⚖

Critical-Circuit Panels

Don't need the whole home covered? We install a critical-load sub-panel that routes only essential circuits through the generator — a cost-effective option for most homes.

🔌

Portable Generator Hook-Up

Already have a portable generator? We install a properly wired inlet box and interlock kit so you can plug in safely — no back-feeding the grid, no electrocution risk.

📋

Maintenance & Load Testing

Generators need annual exercise and load testing. We service existing systems, replace batteries, test the ATS, and document everything so you know it'll work when you need it.
