What we do in Monument

Electrical services for Monument’s discerning homeowners

🔌

EV Charger Install

Level 2 charger installs for Tesla, Ford, GM, and all brands. Hardwired or NEMA 14-50. Garage or exterior. El Paso County permit included.

⚡

Panel Upgrades

200A and 400A service upgrades for larger Monument homes. Sub-panels for detached garages and outbuildings. Fully permitted and inspected.

💡

Lighting

Recessed lighting, under-cabinet, landscape, smart switches, and ceiling fans. We design lighting that enhances Monument's high-quality homes.

📌

Dedicated Circuits

Hot tubs, steam showers, EV chargers, home theaters, and shop tools — every high-draw appliance needs its own circuit. We run them correctly.

📌

Generator & Backup Power

Standby generator installs with automatic transfer switches. Monument's elevation means winter outages happen — be prepared automatically.

🔎

Troubleshooting

Tripping breakers, dead outlets, flickering lights — $65 service call, diagnostic included. Fixed same day when possible.
